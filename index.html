Four maps. One passport.

🗺️ Atlas

A clean, flat world map with country names in your language — fully offline, zoom from the whole world down to a single region. Tap any country to stamp it.

📜 Paper

The same passport, drawn as an aged atlas — burgundy stamps pressed into parchment. Your travel history as it deserves to look.

🧭 Modern & Satellite

Apple Maps styles with your visited countries inked over them, place pins, trip routes and photo markers — all in sync with the Atlas.

🏙️ Cities

7,000+ notable cities worldwide. Check them off by hand — or let your trips seal them automatically as evidence.

Your history builds itself

📸 From your photos

Voymark reads locations and dates from the photos you allow — entirely on your device — and reconstructs trips, places and country stamps. Nothing is ever uploaded.

✍️ Or by hand

Add trips and places with a searchable country list and a tap-on-the-map pin. No coordinates, no forms that fight you. Your manual entries are sacred — no rebuild ever touches them.

🛂 Passport rules

Count the world your way: 197, 193 UN members, or all 249 countries and territories. Airport dashes ink the map without claiming the country — your call, always.

📚 Storytelling

Annual recaps, route replays, transport stats, share cards for any year, and PDF travel books generated from your own history.

Compare passports

Show a friend your QR code — or send a tiny passport file — and see both journeys on one three-color map: only you, only them, and everywhere you've both been. No accounts. Nothing leaves the two phones.

Private by architecture

  • Offline-first. Maps, cities, collections — everything ships inside the app. No tile servers, no API calls.
  • On-device only. Photo scanning happens on your iPhone; images are never copied or uploaded.
  • No account. No tracking. No ads. Backups and exports are files you create and keep.
  • Free. Every feature. We'll never take away what you already have.
